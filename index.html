1. Overview

Food Expiry Reminder is an Android app that helps users save food items, track expiry dates, and receive reminders before food expires.

The app is designed to work offline. It does not require account registration, login, a backend server, or cloud sync.

Data collection No personal data collected
Data sharing No data shared
Storage Stored locally on device

2. Information handled by the app

Food Expiry Reminder does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information with us or with third parties.

The app stores the information entered by the user locally on the user’s own device so the app can display food items, calculate expiry status, and show reminders.

2.1 Food item data stored locally

Data type Purpose Storage location
Food item name To identify the food item inside the app. Locally on the device.
Expiry date To calculate whether the item is active, expiring soon, or expired. Locally on the device.
Reminder date and reminder preference To decide when the app should include the item in local reminder notifications. Locally on the device.
Storage location To help users organize items by fridge, freezer, pantry, or other locations. Locally on the device.
Category To help users organize food items when this field is used. Locally on the device.
Quantity To help users remember the amount of an item when this field is used. Locally on the device.
Notes To allow users to save optional notes about a food item. Locally on the device.
Product image URI To display an optional image selected by the user. Locally on the device.
Item status To show whether an item is active, expiring soon, expired, consumed, or archived. Locally on the device.
Created, updated, consumed, archived, and last notification timestamps To manage item history, status, and reminder behavior. Locally on the device.

2.2 App preference data stored locally

Preference Purpose Storage location
Selected language To display the app in the language selected by the user. Locally on the device.
Onboarding completion status To avoid showing onboarding repeatedly after the user has completed it. Locally on the device.

3. Local storage

The app stores food item data and app preferences locally on the user’s device using local Android storage technologies.

This means the data is used by the app on the same device and is not uploaded by Food Expiry Reminder to our servers.

Because the app is offline-first and does not use a backend account system, uninstalling the app or clearing app data may remove locally stored information from the device.

4. Permissions

Food Expiry Reminder requests only the permissions needed to support local reminders and reminder reliability.

Permission Purpose Required for app usage?
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used on Android 13 and above to show food expiry reminder notifications. No. If denied, the app can still be used, but reminders may not appear.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Used to reschedule local reminder checks after the device restarts. Yes for reminder reliability after device restart.

For product images, the app is designed to use the Android Photo Picker where available. Product images are optional and remain local on the device.

5. Notifications

Food Expiry Reminder uses local notifications to remind users before food items expire.

The app may perform a daily local reminder check at the configured reminder time. If one or more items need attention, the app may show a summary notification.

Notifications are generated locally on the device. The app does not send food item data to a server to create notifications.

Notification delivery can be affected by Android battery saver, Doze Mode, App Standby, manufacturer restrictions, user-disabled notifications, or the device being powered off.

6. Third-party services, analytics, and advertising

Food Expiry Reminder does not use third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or third-party crash reporting SDKs in the described MVP behavior.

The app does not show ads, does not use subscriptions, and does not require online accounts.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties.

The app does not upload food item details, product images, notes, preferences, or notification data to our servers.

Since the app is designed to work without a backend, we do not receive the local food item data stored inside the app.

8. Device backup

Android devices may offer system-level backup features, such as Google device backup, depending on the user’s device settings and Google account settings.

If Android system backup is enabled on the user’s device, some app data may be included in device backups handled by Android or Google. Food Expiry Reminder does not operate those backups and does not use them as an app cloud sync service.

Users can manage device backup behavior through their Android device settings and Google account settings.

9. Data retention and deletion

Food item data and app preferences remain stored locally on the user’s device until the user edits, archives, deletes, consumes items, clears app data, or uninstalls the app.

Because the app does not use an online account or backend database, we do not have access to remotely delete local user data from our servers.

Users can delete app data by deleting items inside the app where available, clearing the app’s storage from Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

10. Children’s privacy

Food Expiry Reminder is a general utility app for managing food expiry reminders. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

The app does not require account registration and does not collect personal information from users.

11. Security

Food Expiry Reminder reduces privacy risk by keeping app data local on the user’s device and by avoiding account registration, backend storage, analytics, advertising SDKs, and third-party data sharing.

Users should still protect their device with appropriate device-level security, such as a passcode, fingerprint, or other screen lock method.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the app’s features, data handling, permissions, or legal requirements change.

When we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.
